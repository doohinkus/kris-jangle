#   carol-courier/build/host/carol-courier_host carol-courier/tests/basic.txt carol-courier/build/host
# Levels build cumulatively: level 1 is 3 notes (64,64,67), level 2 adds the
# next 3 (64,64,64) on top, and level 11 is the whole 32-note song.
# Demo timing is rhythmic now (EIGHTH_MS 180, CAROL_DUR eighths per note,
# plus a 200 ms phrase breath): the Jingle Bells level-1 notes are 2+2+6
# eighths, so LISTEN takes ~400+1800 ms; level 2 is 2+2+6+1+2+2 eighths
# plus one phrase breath, ~400+2700+200 ms. The waits below carry that margin.
